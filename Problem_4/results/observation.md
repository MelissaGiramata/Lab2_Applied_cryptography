# Observation: Percentage of Primes Up to N

The percentage falls fastest at small N, from 13.38% at N = 5,000 to about 9% by N = 200,000, and falls much more slowly after about 1,000,000, still declining to 6.97% at N = 5,000,000. The measured line stays above 100 / ln N, as I predicted: the gap shrinks from 1.64 to 0.49 percentage points and the ratio of measured to 100 / ln N shrinks from 1.140 to 1.075, so the two lines converge slowly. This matches Problem 3: if the prime density near N is about 1 / ln N, the average number of draws until a prime is about ln N. For RSA, primes get rarer only logarithmically (about 1 in 710 random numbers near 2^1024), so finding a large prime just takes more tries rather than being impractical, though it needs a faster primality test than trial division.
